The Replay Layer

The v2 workflow never persisted its posterior. Every sensitivity its two governing priors invite therefore cost a full NUTS run — which is why, across eight releases, none of them was ever run. This is the change that makes the forecast and decision layers replayable off one fit in seconds, and what that buys once they are.

§ Why

Three findings, one shape

The post-run analysis of run 448e7f055ef3 reached the same conclusion three ways. Each is a question that can only be answered by running one fit many times with a single thing changed.

Ranking

Both cures shared the disease

The shipped tail_risk collapses to its volatility floor for every name the book selects. Its proposed replacement, downside_dev, correlates 0.9948 with what it replaced and selects denominators two orders of magnitude below the universe median. Ranking on reward-per-risk selects on the absence of modelled risk.

Sizing

A pin reading as a recommendation

kelly_fraction sat at exactly 1.0 for 89.3% of the universe. That is the bisection reporting that E[log(1+f·r)] never turned over — no simulated scenario loses money. A statement about the forward simulation's left tail, wearing the name of a position size.

Precedence

Two books, no declaration

One posterior produced two sized books sharing 8 of 25 names — one at effective N 24.2 across eight sectors, one at 11.8 with 60.9% in a single sector. Nothing in the export said which was the recommendation, and nothing consumed either.

Every gate in this pipeline scores the model against the analyst trail it was fitted to. No internal check can distinguish a favourable modelled tail from an optimistic one — which is why nothing below moves a default.
§F1

The handoff

A compact NetCDF carrying the four posterior quantities the forward simulation actually reads — deliberately not the raw InferenceData. It is written after the screen, because one of those four is the decision latent and it must be the shrunk one the screen reported. Persisting the ingredients to re-derive it would let a replay describe a different model while every gate still passed.

Thinned to the scenario count on the way out, by a seeded random choice without replacement — the flattened sample axis is chain-major, so a stride would sample chains unevenly and quietly narrow the very between-chain spread the forecast exists to carry. The thinning factor is logged and stamped, because a silently thinned artifact is exactly what a later reader mistakes for the fit itself.

Verified on a synthetic fit: the replay reproduces the live path to the float32 storage tolerance and no further.

§15b

Three arms, no default moved

Two candidate ranking denominators have now shipped and failed the same way. The third candidate is structurally different: a probability is bounded, so it cannot be inflated by a vanishing denominator. Whether it is better is a question about realised returns, so all three ship as labelled arms and every exported row carries which one produced it.

ArmDenominatorKnown failure modeRole
reward_to_downside downside_dev Vanishes; ρ 0.9948 with what it replaced SHIPPED DEFAULT
reward_to_cvar max(−er_p05, k·er_sd, floor) Collapses to its volatility floor for the whole book CONTRAST

The tie-break is not optional

With the majority of a universe sitting at exactly 1.0, a top-k cut lands entirely inside the tie and argsort's ordering silently becomes the selection rule. Ranking runs on (p_upside_pos_cond, shrink_gain, expected_return) and the span of the tie is exported.

Measured at 62% saturation: the cut fell among 186 tied names, and selection is now invariant to input row order.

Two floors, and one of them excludes

The absolute floor already excluded denominators at zero. The problem was the band just above it, which the floor admits and the ranking then rewards. A relative floor — a fraction of the eligible universe's median — sits beside it, and it excludes rather than clamps: clamping would admit exactly the names the floor exists to keep out.

Off by default at q = 0.0, so the shipped arm is bit-identical. rank_denominator_pctile makes the diagnosis a column.

§14b

The counterweight

The v1 workflow ends with a section that turns a posterior into a posture — group over/underweights, an action list, a size-down watch. v2 has no equivalent at all. It is numerically richer and rhetorically empty: it emits ranked frames and never says what to do with them. That logic is now a shared module returning DataFrames, because a console block cannot be exported, diffed, gated or plotted — which is why a pipeline tracking twenty-five gate verdicts tracks zero postures.

A reward-to-risk ratio lets thin evidence inflate a score. The group signals are the one place in the pipeline where thin evidence pulls a signal toward zero. Same input condition, opposite treatment — which is why the two are drawn side by side.
Measured on a synthetic panel with one deliberately noisy group. The group with the largest raw excess receives no verdict; a group with a smaller one, resolved four times better, does.

Confidence is an argument

v1 conditions every probability on the posterior achieve_prob. v2 removed that variable — a sigmoid of a standardised log-uplift is the probability of no defined event. The quantity carrying that meaning in v2 is shrink_gain.

So the module takes confidence as a parameter rather than reading a posterior. Neither caller has to know about the other's variable, and a default of 1.0 makes the conditioning inert and says so.

A veto orthogonal to the ranking

The size-down watch flags a wide posterior or an analyst panel of two. Those are precisely the conditions that produce a forward simulation with no credible left tail — which is what both books select on.

Shipped off: applying it changes which names a book holds. Measuring it is not optional, and the gate reports the informative number in both directions, since once applied the overlap is zero by construction.

§P

Running it

The replay reuses the v2 workflow's vocabulary rather than copying it — the gate report, the identity block, the provenance SSOT and the artifact layout all come from there.

# replay the layers off one fit — seconds, no sampling, no database write
python kalman_portfolio.py --handoff …/07_forecast_handoff_v2.nc

# three books from one posterior, and both prior sweeps on the same fit
python kalman_portfolio.py --rank-arms all --sweep factor_share,multiplier

# take the concentration and the veto deliberately rather than by omission
python kalman_portfolio.py --sector-cap 0.30 --size-down-veto

Nine gates, and every one of them is non-blocking. That is a position rather than caution: each measures either the consequence of a prior nothing identifies, or a property of a forward tail nothing has validated. A threshold on the first would test only that the prior was applied; a threshold on the second would assert the very thing that is unknown.

Colour, measured

The design system is the project's existing semantic roles, not a second palette. Run through the accessibility validator against the real dark surface with all pairs in play: the four-slot set fails at CVD ΔE 7.6, inside the band that is legal only with secondary encoding. Three slots pass every gate — worst CVD ΔE 9.6, normal-vision 20.0, contrast ≥ 3:1.

So the categorical set is capped at three, which is exactly the number of ranking arms. A fourth series folds into “Other”; it is never a generated hue.

What rendering caught

The validator checks colour, not layout. Four defects surfaced only by generating the panels and looking at them: an amber collision that made both legend swatches identical and lost arm identity; y-labels clipped to npany 407; percent ticks collapsing to a column of 0%; and a caption asserting the previous run's finding regardless of the data.

That last one matters most. A figure repeating a conclusion from a different fit is the exact failure this layer exists to prevent, so the headline is now conditional and states which case it is reporting.

§ Not done

Deliberately out of scope

The vintage capture

It remains the top item and this work does not do it. Every gate above is still internal, and the forward tail both books select on stays unvalidated until realised returns exist. What the handoff's provenance stamping does add is the piece that makes a later vintage attributable to the code that produced it.

Mean-model arms from a replay

Max-and-Smooth screens mean-structure arms in seconds against a frozen covariance, and that freeze is a feature — it isolates the mean-structure effect. But it freezes exactly the parameters the forward simulation reads, and the handoff carries the four quantities the simulator needs, not the model graph. The stage refuses rather than silently degrading into a comparison of one arm against itself.

Writing to the analytics schema

The replay exports CSV. The v2 tables are DROP-and-RECREATE, so a replay that wrote into them would destroy the export it was replaying. Precedence gets declared in the v2 DDL first.
